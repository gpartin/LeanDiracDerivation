# V3.5 Covariant Observable Closure Plan

## Requested finish line

Starting from the unchanged cumulative V3.4 theorem, close the remaining
free, covariant spin-one-half representation gaps that can be settled without
packet transport, interactions, quantization, or a physical-unit anchor.
V3.5 must treat both energy branches symmetrically, extend covariance from
individual generators to generated transformations, expose the complete
Dirac bilinear family, connect moving helicity to the invariant spin
classification, and publish invariant acceptance tests for the separate V4
transport program.

V3.5 changes no LFM action, governing equation, C19 geometry, coefficient,
vacuum, readout, or physical scale. Every new operator is defined from the
already-derived cubical alpha, beta, gamma, spin, boost, and spectral
operators.
