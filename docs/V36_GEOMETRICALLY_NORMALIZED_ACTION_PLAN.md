# V3.6 Geometrically Normalized Action Plan

## Requested finish line

Replace the live research action's unsupported relative `Psi`/`chi` kinetic
normalization with the value implied when both native cubical grades inherit
the same standard Euclidean Clifford metric. The oriented cube Hodge map must
be explicit and norm preserving. The action must therefore use `B = 1` and,
under the inherited relation `B * kappa = chi0`, `kappa = 19`.

Then rebuild the complete V2--V3.5 free-Dirac theorem chain under that revised
foundation. Keep `(kappa,B)=(1/63,1197)` and `(1,19)` only as named controls.
Do not promote this research action to repository-wide canonical v40.1 or
claim nonlinear electron, transport, or gravity closure.

## Design red-team preflight

### Requested finish line

One cumulative Lean theorem must show that the action at the root of this
derivation now uses the equal-grade coefficient, that all inherited free
Dirac results still compile, and that the old coefficient was not smuggled
back into the proof. The geometric scope of the selection must be explicit.

### Required pass gates

1. `Foundation.lean` sets the live research values to `B = 1` and
   `kappa = 19`.
2. `NativeAction.lean` exposes the V3.6 C19 Lagrangian and Hamiltonian with
   the live value `B = 1`.
3. The oriented cube vector-to-bivector Hodge map and inverse are explicit,
   and Euclidean norm equality is proved.
4. `B = 1` is unique within the stated common inherited Euclidean metric
   class; no universal uniqueness claim is made over every possible action.
5. The old pairs `(1/63,1197)` and `(1,19)` remain controls and are not used
   as the live foundation.
6. Every pair satisfies the bookkeeping relation `B * kappa = chi0`.
7. The existing arbitrary-`B` transverse tangent theorem is specialized to
   the revised live action and both controls, with the same `B`-free Dirac
   limit.
8. The cumulative V2--V3.5 theorem chain recompiles from the revised
   foundation rather than being copied or assumed.
9. A common cubical turn is proved to preserve the kinetic form for arbitrary
   `B`, so rotational invariance alone is not misreported as the selector.
10. The inherited radial potential is used to derive the different nonlinear
    response consequences of the three pairs.
11. The cumulative build has no unfinished proof, custom axiom, opaque
    constant, fitted coefficient, measured electron input, or SI anchor.

### Disallowed live mechanisms

- selecting `B = 1` because it improves a mobility or stability experiment;
- calling the number 63 a kinetic theorem merely because it counts modes;
- claiming cube symmetry alone forces equal kinetic weights;
- identifying the two physical fields merely because their cubical grades
  have an isometric coordinate representation;
- importing Einstein's coupling, Newton's constant, electron mass, or an SI
  calibration;
- claiming a localized electron, nonlinear transport, gravity closure, or
  promotion of this research action to canonical v40.1.

### Planned live mechanisms

- one standard Euclidean Clifford kinetic metric for the vector and bivector
  grades used in the existing cubical representation;
- the explicit cube Hodge identification `(x,y,z) -> (z,-y,x)` in plane
  order `(01,02,12)`;
- the inherited arbitrary-`B` finite-periodic transverse tangent theorem;
- the existing oriented exterior product and C19 interaction geometry;
- the inherited radial potential and exact polynomial algebra;
- explicit historical/ablation controls for the two rejected normalizations.

### Claim boundary

Passing V3.6 proves a revised *research action*: within the explicitly chosen
common Euclidean cubical Clifford kinetic metric, corresponding vector and
bivector unit blades have equal weight, so the relative coefficient is
uniquely `B = 1`; the inherited parameter relation then gives `kappa = 19`.
It also proves that the complete formal free-Dirac chain survives this action
revision and that the free transverse result itself cannot distinguish the
three tested values.

It does not prove that a common Euclidean metric is the only possible kinetic
architecture, that the revised nonlinear theory has a source-free electron,
that it transports one, or that it produces Einstein gravity. Those are
independent validation obligations. The repository-wide canonical v40.1
action is not changed by this research branch.
