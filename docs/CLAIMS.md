# Claims and boundaries

## Proved

Starting from the declared two-field C19 action and one exact zero-energy
vacuum representative, the complete transverse linear phase space has a
bijection to four complex components whose first-order evolution is the free
Dirac equation in the low-momentum/continuum limit. The mass, three transport
directions, time map, density, and current are derived through one consistent
readout. Finite-C19 and centered-time corrections remain explicit and vanish
in the proved limits. Both cubical rank-four copies are retained.

The cumulative theorem and every transitive dependency compile without
`sorry`, custom axioms, or assumed Dirac matrices.

## Not claimed

- a derivation of the native action itself;
- dynamic cosmological selection of one vacuum orientation;
- a nonlinear localized electron or its motion;
- electromagnetic interaction or later particle properties;
- exact continuum symmetry at finite lattice spacing.

Those are separate questions and are not premises of the free-equation
derivation.
