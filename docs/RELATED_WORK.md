# Related work

The cubical incidence construction belongs to the Dirac--Kahler and discrete
differential-form lineage. Relevant background includes:

- P. Becher and H. Joos, *The Dirac--Kahler equation and fermions on the lattice*, 1982.
- J. M. Rabin, *Homology theory of lattice fermion doubling*, 1982.
- F. L. Teixeira, *Differential Forms in Lattice Field Theories: An Overview*, 2013.

This repository's narrower contribution is a machine-checked chain from its
declared complex-Psi/complex-chi C19 action to a free linearized Dirac
equation with explicit spatial, temporal, density, and current remainders. It
does not claim to originate the general lattice Clifford construction.
