DIRAC-029 PASS: the antiparticle branch is already inside the free operator

The four-component sector does not contain only positive-frequency electron
solutions. DIRAC-029 derives its conjugate partner without adding another
field or equation.

Lean built the conjugation operation from three things the chain already had:
ordinary complex conjugation, the mass grading and the y-direction transport
operator. It proved that applying this operation twice returns the original
state, that the state's density is unchanged, and that

    positive energy at momentum p
      becomes
    negative energy at momentum -p.

This works in both of the cubical flavor copies; neither copy was thrown away
or simply renamed as antimatter. The result is the free Dirac antiparticle
structure. Identifying electric charge and an interacting positron will need
an interaction theorem later, so this stage does not assume either one.

Next, DIRAC-030 stays with the isolated electron and asks how its conserved
current separates into ordinary flow and an intrinsic spin circulation. That
is the Gordon decomposition.
