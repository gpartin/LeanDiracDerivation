The eight vertices of an oriented cube carry three signed incidence
operations, one for each coordinate direction, together with the cube's parity
grading.  This submission proves that the associated first-order complex
symbol squares to the isotropic quadratic symbol selected by the
long-wavelength second moment of the C19 cubic stencil.  The result is an
algebraic factorization statement; it does not claim a localized electron,
physical spin, or an exact finite-spacing Dirac equation.
