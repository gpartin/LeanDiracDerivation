  quadratic symbol; DIRAC-023 does not supply a new dispersion relation.

  current velocity proved in DIRAC-013/019.

  plane-wave phase is evaluated along the independently derived transport
  trajectory, and Lean first derives its rate as `chi0^2/E`.
- Directional phase velocity is not left as an unexplained quotient: Lean
  proves it keeps the corresponding plane-wave phase fixed.
- The clock-rate square-root law and energy/clock reciprocity are consequences
  of that phase rate and the prior dispersion theorem.
- The zitter amplitude-frequency lock is the unchanged DIRAC-022 theorem; it
  is surfaced here because it was previously underreported, not rederived by
  fitting a new scale.

## Controls

The independent reconstruction samples rest, axial, oblique, signed and
higher-momentum cases. A 10% change in the current-velocity coefficient breaks
the group/phase relation, and replacing the action-selected rest frequency 19
with 18 breaks energy/clock reciprocity. Nonzero momentum is required only
where phase velocity divides by that component. All evidence is finite and
the maximum admitted reconstruction error is below `2e-11`.

## Claim boundary

The result holds for the previously derived low-momentum continuum sector. It
does not establish exact finite-spacing phase harmony, assign an SI electron
mass or clock, prove a localized nonlinear native electron, derive unrestricted
live-chi electron motion, or provide electromagnetic response or a `g` factor.
