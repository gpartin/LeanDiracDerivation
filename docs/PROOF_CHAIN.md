## Current theorem boundary

DIRAC-015 proves a conditional free-Dirac continuum sector of the declared
two-field action. Restricting and varying that action is proved to commute for
the retained eight-site embedding. The action-derived tangent has the massive
C19 Klein--Gordon symbol. The derived four-component Hamiltonian squares to its
continuum part. The centered recurrence itself supplies a first-order branch
under the stated stability bound; its exact native defect is the finite-spacing C19 remainder,
and its generator coefficients converge to those of the same Dirac Hamiltonian. The
same action supplies the admitted density/current limit. Both rank-four copies
are retained. DIRAC-016 proves that the existing unsigned period-two cube
translation exchanges those copies in one coordinate direction and returns
after a second translation. It does not identify that permutation with one
native timestep or with the motion of a localized carrier. DIRAC-017 proves
that the same signed cube-incidence algebra carries a spin-one-half rotation
representation without inserting a spin matrix or half-angle rule. The exact
standard-spin intertwiner, component spectrum, continuous unitary rotations,
mass grading, and quarter-turn plus all-angle interpreted-Hamiltonian
covariance are all part of the certificate. It does not prove that
unrestricted native time evolution makes a localized stable carrier realize
that representation. DIRAC-018 proves that the orientation is forced inside
the stated real local flavor-preserving cubical-incidence class, using the
mass/current sign already derived upstream. It does not extend that
classification to arbitrary nonlocal or flavor-mixing roots, nor derive
electromagnetic coupling or a magnetic `g` factor. This does not prove
unrestricted uniqueness, exact finite-lattice Dirac
dynamics, arbitrary nonlinear live-chi closure, electromagnetic coupling,
operational spin of a localized carrier, or a physical electron.
DIRAC-019 adds sustained transport on the positive-frequency spectral branch,
using the same recurrence, Hamiltonian, density and current. DIRAC-020 turns
that finite mode family into a coherent field through an explicit normalized
finite Fourier frame, proves the cubic character-to-momentum phase bridge,
proves exact Parseval density and integrated-current
identities, and retains a finite-update pointwise error bound. It does not yet
provide a uniform fixed-physical-time estimate carrying the exact native C19
spatial remainder, or prove nonlinear electron motion in unrestricted live
chi.
DIRAC-021 then specializes the already-derived Hamiltonian to zero momentum.
It proves both rest-energy signs and their exact time equations, derives the
relative phase from the quotient of those branches, and fixes the native rest
and branch-coherence frequencies and periods. It does not claim that a pure
global phase is observable. DIRAC-022 derives the missing free-continuum cross-branch observable from the
same Hamiltonian, velocity and phases. It does not identify that expectation
value with motion of a localized nonlinear native lattice carrier. DIRAC-023
then follows the existing plane-wave phase along the current-selected packet
trajectory and derives phase harmony, reciprocal energy/clock ratios, and the
standard relativistic clock slowdown. These are controlled continuum-sector
identities; no exact finite-spacing, SI-calibrated, electromagnetic, or
localized nonlinear-electron claim is added.
DIRAC-024 then proves the projective endpoint bridge between the independently
derived zitter beat and cubical spinor rotation. It does not identify their
generators, claim that zitterbewegung causes spin, or establish a localized
nonlinear carrier.
DIRAC-025 then derives a longitudinal velocity variance from the existing
Hamiltonian response and proves its normalized rms equals the independently
derived moving clock factor. It does not reinterpret the operator spectrum as
a classical trajectory or extend the result beyond the controlled continuum
sector.
