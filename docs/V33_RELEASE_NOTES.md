# V3.3 Research Release Notes

V3.3 is a cumulative theorem-only extension of V3.2. It adds no action term,
field, coefficient, stencil, vacuum, or physical scale.

New machine-checked results:

- complete orthonormal four-component basis and outer-product resolution;
- constructed positive/negative spectral states, eigenvalue equations,
  branch disjointness, and exact spectral completeness;
- normalized scalar, density, and current bilinears with native-action
  provenance;
- exact axis-aligned moving helicity states with eigenvalues `-1/2` and `1/2`;
- forward equal-momentum Gordon time and spatial relations;
- a two-sided free momentum-space resolvent away from its pole;
- a cumulative `v33_complete_free_spinor` certificate.

The release still does not prove a physical electron, electromagnetic
coupling, `g = 2`, charge quantization, transport, localization, or conversion
of native mass `19` into MeV. Those boundaries are deliberate.
