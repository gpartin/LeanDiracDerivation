The key distinction is between a derivation *inside a selected kinetic metric
architecture* and a universal uniqueness theorem. V3.6 removes the
unjustified factor of 63 from the live research action because no theorem in
the Dirac chain connects that count to the `chi` kinetic metric. It chooses
the parsimonious common Euclidean Clifford metric already used to compare the
vector and bivector grades. Lean proves the resulting coefficient and its
Dirac no-regression consequences. Lean does not prove that no other kinetic
architecture could be proposed or that the revised nonlinear theory is
already physically validated.
