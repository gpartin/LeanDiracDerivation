The eight complex amplitudes on an oriented cube admit a geometric involution
built from two signed corner flips.  This submission proves that every
eight-corner amplitude decomposes into its positive and negative eigensectors,
that the sectors intersect only at zero, and that the cubical C19 first-order
Dirac symbol preserves both sectors.  Thus the full eight-corner fiber is
exhausted by two disjoint four-component sectors carrying the same free
first-order generator.  This is an algebraic classification result; it does
not claim a nonlinear localized electron or experimental validation.
