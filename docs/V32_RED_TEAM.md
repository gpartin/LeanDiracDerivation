## RCA

The original 17-stage plan bundled several research-sized theorems into single
stages. Creating a certificate around existing V3.1 results was enough to make
the module compile but not enough to satisfy the prose. Strengthening Stage 2
then exposed the deeper issue: the exact action symbol is trigonometric while
the existing local cubical Dirac root squares to its quadratic continuum
symbol. Lean proves equality exactly when the C19 spatial remainder is zero,
and proves an explicit momentum where it is not zero. No proof tactic can
repair that mismatch while preserving the unchanged operator and action.

Permitted next choices are therefore outside the approved V3.2 work order:

1. narrow V3.2 to an exact continuum-root theorem with a controlled finite-C19
   remainder; or
2. authorize a new geometry-derived finite-lattice first-order operator and
   prove its locality, Hermiticity, spectrum, and no-regression properties.

The follow-up factorization investigation strengthens this conclusion. Lean
proves that **no finite sum of squares of real, odd, Brillouin-periodic
transport coefficients** can equal the exact C19 symbol: all such coefficients
vanish at `(pi,0,0)`, while C19 equals `48` there. Lean also proves that C19
does have an exact local decomposition into its eighteen complex weighted bond
differences. Turning those bond channels into a first-order state operator
would enlarge the state/readout and is therefore new framework research, not
an unchanged-action V3.2 repair. See `V32_EXACT_FACTOR_INVESTIGATION.md`.

Route 1 is now authorized. The release gate remains closed only for the other
open stage gaps in this table; the Stage-2 incompatibility is no longer a
release blocker because it is explicitly part of the narrowed claim.
