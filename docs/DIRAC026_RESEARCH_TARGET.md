# DIRAC-026 research target: derived magnetic response

## Objective

Determine whether a uniquely specified, reciprocal external phase probe of
the already-derived continuum sector yields the standard Dirac magnetic
moment and `g = 2` coefficient without inserting a Pauli term or fitting the
answer.

## Mandatory safeguards

- Derive any probe coupling from the same phase symmetry and action/current
  map; do not install a magnetic-moment operator as a native law.
- Keep the two native fields, C19 weights, action and update unchanged unless
  a separately reviewed reciprocal action extension is explicitly declared.
- Derive the low-field coefficient by reduction of the probed Dirac operator,
  not by defining `g` or a Pauli term.
- Retain both cubical copies and all sign conventions.
- Include zero-field, charge-sign and wrong-coefficient controls.
- Do not claim the electron charge, SI magneton or interacting native
  electromagnetism unless those inputs are independently derived.

## Promotion criterion

Promote only if Lean proves the coefficient from the admitted probe and the
independent reconstruction rejects inserted-coefficient alternatives. If the
phase probe is not uniquely fixed by the existing framework, record the gate
as open rather than choosing one for the desired result.
