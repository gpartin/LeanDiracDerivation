## Claim boundary used for every submission

V2 is a machine-checked derivation of the complete free linearized Dirac
operator from the declared reciprocal two-field C19 lattice action about an
exact native zero-energy vacuum, with explicit lattice and timestep
remainders and two exhaustive rank-four cubical sectors. It is not a proof
that nature uses this substrate, a nonlinear localized-electron theorem, an
origin story for vacuum selection, or experimental validation.
