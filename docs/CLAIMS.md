# Claims and boundaries

## Proved

Starting from the declared two-field C19 action and one exact zero-energy
vacuum representative, the complete transverse linear phase space has a
bijection to four complex components whose first-order evolution is the free
Dirac equation in the low-momentum/continuum limit. The mass, three transport
directions, time map, density, and current are derived through one consistent
readout. Finite-C19 and centered-time corrections remain explicit and vanish
in the proved limits. The cubical eight-corner fiber is proved to decompose
exhaustively into exactly two disjoint rank-four sectors, and both sectors
carry the same free Dirac generator.

The existence of those two sectors is a formal prediction of the declared
cubical root. The additional interpretation that a physical electron
measurement accesses one sector without revealing which sector was accessed
is a proposed physical reading, not a Lean theorem.

The cumulative theorem and every transitive dependency compile without
`sorry`, custom axioms, or assumed Dirac matrices.

The free transverse reduction is uniform in the substrate kinetic parameter
`B`. In particular, the proof does not use the numerical value `B = 1197` to
obtain the free-Dirac mass, Clifford transport, or current. This is a scope
result, not a claim that `B` is irrelevant to the full theory: it continues to
control live-`chi` inertia outside this decoupled linear sector.

On V3 `main`, the same incidence-derived operators
also determine a unique nonzero spin normalization.  The resulting operators
have projections `+/-1/2`, Casimir `3/4`, preserve the positive four-component
density, reverse sign after a `2*pi` rotation, and return after `4*pi`.  The
derived rotation and boost generators satisfy the Lorentz Lie algebra and act
covariantly on the oriented gamma four-vector.  Both cubical copies carry this
same representation.  No spin matrix or half-angle is assumed independently;
the half scale is the unique nonzero solution of the native closure equation.

## Not claimed

- a derivation of the native action itself;
- dynamic cosmological selection or an origin story for one vacuum
  orientation (explicitly outside the target theorem);
- derivation of the chi kinetic normalization `B = 1197`, or a proof that this
  value is required for free Dirac emergence;
- a nonlinear localized electron or its motion;
- electromagnetic interaction or later particle properties;
- exact continuum symmetry at finite lattice spacing;
- a nonlinear localized carrier realizing operational Stern--Gerlach spin;
- electromagnetic coupling, a magnetic moment, or a `g`-factor calculation.

Those are separate questions and are not premises of the free-equation
derivation.

## V4 proved boundary

V4 proves that the unchanged action admits an exact fixed-center localized
two-field orbit on every sufficiently large periodic C19 box. The native force
closes on the eight-site carrier plus live first-shell `chi` field, ordinary
vacuum remains outside, and the synchronous centered update advances the state
without runtime support.

This does not yet prove that the orbit is stable, that its internal coordinates
are the V3 four-component spinor, or that it translates with the V3 Dirac
current. Those are explicit open obligations rather than inherited claims.
