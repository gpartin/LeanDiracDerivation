# Palomar literature review

Search date: 2026-11-14

## Scope

This search was performed to position the submitted conditional theorem, not
to claim priority. The formal result concerns a prepared uniform-background
tangent of a two-field C19 action, an eight-corner signed-incidence square
root of its quadratic continuum symbol, explicit finite-spacing and
centered-time corrections, and a tangent continuity law.

## Search method

The search covered publisher pages, DOI records, and arXiv using combinations
of these terms:

- lattice Dirac operator and continuum limit;
- cubical or hypercubic Dirac factorization;
- Dirac--Kahler differential forms and lattice fermions;
- staggered fermions and signed lattice incidence;
- Nielsen--Ninomiya fermion doubling;
- discrete-time quantum walk and Dirac convergence;
- finite-difference Dirac/Klein--Gordon factorization.

The search was targeted rather than systematic and did not exhaust books,
non-English literature, unpublished notes, or every citation graph. Therefore
historical priority and originality remain unestablished.

## Closest established lines of work

1. F. L. Teixeira, *Differential Forms in Lattice Field Theories: An
   Overview*, DOI 10.1155/2013/487270. This reviews primal/dual complexes,
   discrete exterior calculus, and lattice Dirac--Kahler constructions.
2. H. B. Nielsen and M. Ninomiya, *A No-Go Theorem for Regularizing Chiral
   Fermions*, DOI 10.1016/0370-2693(81)91026-1. This is central background for
   lattice species doubling and forbids stronger chiral interpretations under
   its hypotheses.
3. J. M. Rabin, *Homology Theory of Lattice Fermion Doubling*, DOI
   10.1016/0550-3213(82)90434-5. This gives the geometric/homological context
   for degeneracy in lattice transcriptions of continuum fermions.
4. P. Arrighi, M. Forets, and V. Nesme, *The Dirac Equation as a Quantum Walk:
   Higher Dimensions, Observational Convergence*, arXiv:1307.3524. This proves
   continuum convergence for a different discrete, unitary, first-order
   construction.
5. L. Mlodinow and T. A. Brun, *Discrete Spacetime, Quantum Walks and
   Relativistic Wave Equations*, arXiv:1802.03910. This relates cubic-lattice
   symmetries, internal degrees of freedom, Dirac limits, and leading lattice
   corrections in a quantum-walk setting.

## Position and limitations

The submitted Lean theorem is not presented as the first lattice
Dirac construction, the first square root of a lattice Laplacian, or a
resolution of fermion doubling. Its narrower contribution is a machine-checked
connection among one specified action restriction, one exact C19 symbol, one
concrete eight-corner incidence factor, explicit low-momentum/time error
bounds, and one local phase-continuity identity.

The development retains both rank-four cubical copies. It does not derive a
single chiral fermion, a localized electron, physical spin, a nonlinear
live-background Dirac equation, gauge interactions, or uniqueness. These
boundaries are part of the submission rather than deferred caveats.
