# RCA: why the previous localized route did not complete the free-Dirac proof

Issue: The fixed compact eight-site rest orbit could not supply the rank-four
momentum-dependent operator bridge required for the complete free Dirac
equation.

Why it matters: A rest clock and Clifford factorization alone do not derive
the three signed transport generators.  Without one common embedding for
mass, time, transport, density, and current, the result is only a conditional
factorization and not a native free-Dirac derivation.

Symptoms: The full localized tangent classification found that the exact
rest orbit breaks one-site translation, so it has no native Bloch fiber
operator at fixed momentum.  Every tested compact rank-four candidate failed
the transport test.  The rest-plane response ranks were `(1,1,0)`, and the
first spatial response left the compact carrier into exterior modes rather
than closing on the four retained coordinates.

Expected symptoms: A passing localized bridge would have one invariant
four-complex-dimensional bundle, a nonzero signed derivative in each of the
three axes, a common mass grading, and a Noether current pulled through the
same embedding.

Root cause: A fixed-center localized solution is not translation invariant.
Bloch momentum and its derivatives are native to a translation-invariant
linearization, not to one compact defect held at one center.  The proof was
asking one mathematical object to be both a pinned nonlinear orbit and the
homogeneous free-field carrier of momentum.

Corrective action: Separate the free-field theorem from the localized-
particle identification.  Linearize the unchanged native action about its
own uniform global minimum, derive the two transverse C19 Klein--Gordon
channels, reconstruct their complete four-component phase space, and prove
the Dirac intertwiner with explicit lattice/time corrections.  Preserve the
localized orbit as a later theorem asking whether a nonlinear excitation of
that free field realizes a translating electron.  Do not convert the scoped
localized obstruction into a universal no-go.
