# Dirac motion inverse research

Date: 2026-11-24

Status: `ACTION_PHASE_SPACE_CONNECTION_REQUIRED`

Canonical V40 changed: no

V4-006 closed: no

## Question

Can the movement term of the Dirac equation be used as an inverse diagnostic
without putting a Dirac matrix, selected spinor, preferred direction, or target
trajectory into the native action?

The tested comparator was the independently evolved discrete Dirac operator
built from the exact C19 symbol and the already-derived cubical Clifford
generators. It supplied no force, projection, frame, reset, or fitted runtime
input to the native evolution.

## What the Dirac movement term requires

For each momentum component the Dirac transport generator does two things at
once:

1. it moves amplitude between neighboring carrier centers with a signed,
   direction-dependent response; and
2. it rotates the four retained internal components into the frame appropriate
   to the receiving center.

Rigidly shifting eight corner amplitudes tests only the first operation. A
valid native carrier must also transport its internal frame. This is why an
integer cube hop, scalar stiffness change, or receiver preload is not an
adequate realization of the Dirac transport term.

## Tests

### 1. Frequency-resolved inverse compression

`cube_incidence_dirac_inverse_schur.py` eliminated exterior degrees of freedom
at fixed frequency and compared the retained native response with the C19
Dirac comparator. Exterior dressing cancels much of the normal residual but
does not remove the retained four-component mismatch. The diagnostic action
coefficients are fitted inverse data and were not admitted.

### 2. Localized translation curvature

`cube_incidence_dirac_translation_requirement.py` measured the tangent
curvature along translated copies of the localized rest carrier. The current
action has nonzero generalized curvatures near `0.218` and `0.220`; the earlier
action basis cancels only about one percent. This is evidence of lattice
pinning for the rigid localized family. It is not a no-go for an extended or
relative-periodic moving wave.

### 3. Exceptional position-only C19 actions

`cube_incidence_exceptional_discretization_screen.py` screened 32 local,
reciprocal, cubic-symmetric position-only directions. They include face/edge
redistributions, complex bilinear powers, coherent hopping, density-modulated
hopping, scalar-overlap hopping, and sigma hopping. The rest-preserving
subspace has dimension 18, but its best relative translation residual is
`0.249661`; the unconstrained fit is still `0.162155`. The remaining residual
is overwhelmingly carrier-localized Psi amplitude/phase response.

Simple geometry-derived face/edge cancellation ratios preserve rest but are
almost blind to the missing translation response. Therefore the currently
tested position-only action space cannot close V4-006.

### 4. Alternating center parity

`cube_incidence_staggered_packet_gate.py` tested a beta-alternating carrier
without changing the action. The first native step agrees with the discrete
Dirac comparator at approximately `4.97e-6`, while the one-cycle phase-aligned
error falls to `0.270390`. This is materially better than the historical
all-center packet, but not closed.

The residual has a geometric explanation: C19 face bonds reverse checkerboard
parity, whereas edge bonds preserve it. Since the Dirac transport generator
flips beta grade, a simple two-color staggering treats the face and edge parts
of C19 differently and cannot be exact by itself.

### 5. Both cubical copies

`cube_incidence_copy_exchange_packet_gate.py` constructed an exact Hermitian
copy-exchange involution that squares to one, anticommutes with flavor, and
commutes with beta and all three spatial Clifford generators. Using it to put
the face and edge channels into different copies gives one-cycle error
`0.574070`. The simplest hypothesis that the second copy alone carries the
missing edge transport is therefore rejected. This is scoped to that exact
copy-exchange construction, not a universal no-go for both copies.

### 6. Spin-diagonal local frame

`cube_incidence_spin_diagonal_packet_gate.py` used the standard local Clifford
frame generated by products of the three alpha generators. It did not alter
the action. The best first-step error is `4.56e-6`; the best one-cycle
phase-aligned error is `0.217340`, with norm ratio `0.983435`. Relative to the
historical approximately `0.63` one-cycle shape error, this is about a 65%
reduction.

This result is diagnostic, not a pass. Selecting the best alpha ordering by
outcome is a fit unless the same local frame is derived from the native
Psi--chi state.

### 7. Native frame reconstruction

`cube_incidence_native_frame_connection.py` derived all C19 neighbor overlaps
from the full native Psi and chi carrier modes using their kinetic metric.
The resulting connection has nontrivial holonomy, so native frame information
is present. Some face overlaps are rank deficient, however, making their polar
completion nonunique.

`cube_incidence_native_frame_synchronization.py` then built an unbiased global
synchronization from all 18 C19 overlaps. Its local four-component blocks are
nearly singular and its one-cycle error is `0.5026`. Therefore the native
overlap construction tested here does not derive the successful spin-diagonal
frame.

## Root result

The current action is not failing because it cannot launch a Dirac-like
disturbance. At the first step, the error can already be of order `10^-6`.
It fails because successive sites do not receive a uniquely defined native
four-component frame that makes each later push build on the previous push.
The mismatch accumulates into the in-plane Psi amplitude/phase channel over an
internal cycle.

The action's position-only terms can change stiffness and coherent hopping,
but the tested 32-direction space cannot generate the missing phase-space
rotation while preserving the rest carrier. The second copy and an unbiased
preparation/readout change also fail to supply it. The evidence therefore
supports a missing *native phase-space connection*, not another scalar
stiffness adjustment.

## Admissible repair target

The next action class must be a real, local, reciprocal Psi--chi kinetic or
symplectic connection whose frame is calculated from native complete-frame
quantities such as the scalar overlap `chi^dagger Psi`, the oriented area
`chi wedge Psi`, and their C19 neighbor relations. It must:

- have zero first variation on the admitted rest orbit;
- produce both reciprocal Psi and chi responses from one action;
- be nondegenerate or have a proved constrained Hamiltonian sector;
- reproduce the signed face and edge transport through one geometry-derived
  rule rather than a selected alpha ordering;
- retain both cubical copies;
- close the measured one-cycle residual before any long nonlinear run; and
- derive its coefficient from normalization, geometry, positivity, or a
  native degeneracy condition rather than fitting Dirac velocity.

The appropriate next calculation is a Hessian/rank test of this action class
against the measured spin-diagonal residual. If its admissible response space
does not contain that residual, the whole class should be retired before any
coefficient sweep. If it does, solve the coefficient algebraically, re-solve
the rest carrier, and retest the unchanged discrete Dirac comparator.

## Literature relationship

The diagnosis is consistent with established discrete-wave results:

- exceptional discretizations can remove a Peierls--Nabarro barrier by
  redistributing nonlinear response across bonds, but absence of a barrier
  alone does not guarantee arbitrary-velocity motion;
- Peierls--Nabarro barriers arise when continuous translation is broken by a
  nonlinear lattice;
- Peierls--Nabarro-free discrete nonlinear Schrodinger systems use nonlinear
  coherent hopping rather than scalar onsite stiffness alone; and
- discrete nonlinear Dirac solitary waves naturally exhibit staggered site
  structure and become more mobile in broad, continuum-like regimes.

These analogies motivated the tested bases. They do not prove that the LFM
carrier is an electron or that the proposed action class will pass.
