### Description

Lean 4 derivation of the complete free linearized Dirac equation from a
declared reciprocal complex-Psi/complex-chi C19 lattice action. The formal
chain derives the native action variation, an exact uniform zero-energy
vacuum, the finite-lattice tangent operator, a cubical Clifford square root,
a bijective four-component phase-space readout, rest mass, three transport
generators, the centered-time relation, and the density/current bridge with
explicit spatial and temporal remainders. The eight-corner fiber is proved to
decompose exhaustively into exactly two disjoint rank-four sectors carrying
the same free Dirac generator. The release uses no external or maintained
prepared background. It does not claim a nonlinear localized electron,
vacuum-selection origin story, or experimental validation.
